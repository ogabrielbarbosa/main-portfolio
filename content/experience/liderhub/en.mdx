## The business

LíderHub helps law firms close more contracts. AI agents talk to the firm's clients on WhatsApp, and the firm follows everything in a CRM.

People love the product. It solves such a painful problem that firms feel relief once they start using it, and they use it every day.

## How we built

I joined to help rebuild the product with a strong focus on writing code with AI, since the project came from no-code tools and couldn't handle the scale anymore. The way we built changed along the way: we started with Lovable, moved to Cursor and, once we found Claude Code, moved our development to it.

Besides building, I helped keep the app running and orchestrate the dev team.

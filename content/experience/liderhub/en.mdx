## The business

LiderHub helps law firms be more productive at closing deals. AI agents talk to the firm's clients on WhatsApp and help close and manage contracts.

People love the product. It solves such a painful problem that firms feel relief once they start using it, and they use it every day.

## What I came to do

I joined in July 2025 to help rebuild the product: better technology, a faster app that's easier to use, with a strong focus on building with AI.

The way we built changed along the way. We started with Lovable, moved to Cursor and, once we found Claude Code, moved our development to it.

## My contributions

- **Second version of the app.** Rebuilt the whole support system off no-code, in React + Supabase: chat, messages, search, kanban and contacts. We launched in ~5 weeks and this version reached 1,000+ customers.
- **Realtime.** Found what was blowing up the traffic and moved to broadcast, cutting 95% of events without stopping production.
- **Support.** Moved support out of WhatsApp and Notion into the platform, with trackable tickets, support metrics and incidents with an owner.
- **Onboarding.** Turned a hidden banner into a guided first access, with terms acceptance and call booking.
- **Dev process.** Moved tasks to GitHub Projects, standardized issues and PRs with templates and AI skills, and started leading the daily and the deploys.

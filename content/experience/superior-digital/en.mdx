## The project

I joined Superior Digital to build a hospital's mobile app. Every admitted patient had a phone in their ward, and doctors needed to video call the patient and keep track of how they were doing without going to the bedside.

The backend already existed. Our part was the whole app, written in React Native, back when we didn't have AI helping us code.

## What I built

- **Video calls inside the app.** We integrated an open source video calling library, Zoom or Google Meet style, so doctors could call patients straight from the phone.
- **Follow-up after the call.** The doctor records whether the patient is doing well or not.
- **Medical record.** The visit details are filled into the patient's medical record from the app itself.

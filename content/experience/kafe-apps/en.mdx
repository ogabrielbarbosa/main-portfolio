## What I did

Kafé Apps is a software house that builds products for clients. I joined as a mid-level Bubble developer and worked on three projects at the same time.

My role was to get projects started: set up each one's foundation in Bubble and build the features until they were running. It was a short stint, about five months, but it taught me to split my attention across different clients without letting any of them stall.

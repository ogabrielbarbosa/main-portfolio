## How it started

In 2021, in the last year of my IT technical course, my teacher invited me and my team to build a project for the school itself. It would count as our final project, a research project and our internship.

## The problem

We talked to the school counselor to understand what she needed. When something happened in class, the information took a while to reach her, and each student's history was scattered.

## What we built

We designed and built everything from scratch. We imported the students' enrollment records into our database and built the flow:

1. In class, the teacher opens the app, takes the student's ID card and types in the enrollment number.
2. Logs the incident and, if needed, marks it as a formal warning.
3. The incident reaches the counselor's computer instantly, with all of the student's information and history.
4. The counselor follows up with the student from there and issues the warning.

The school used the system, and signed off on our internship.

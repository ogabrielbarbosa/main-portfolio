## Situation

Until February 2026, LíderHub's tasks sat scattered across Notion: over 2,000 cards, about 40% with no priority, 60% not saying whether they were a bug or a feature, and almost none linked to code. A feature idea was just a checkbox on a page. Issues and PRs followed no standard, and the team had no clear sense of what to do first.

## Task

We were 3 developers and the CTO. I took on organizing the workflow, from what goes into the backlog to what ships to production.

## Action

1. Moved task management to GitHub Projects, with a Backlog → In Dev → Testing → QA → Code Review → Done flow. I wrote most of the issues and assigned them across the team.
2. Created issue templates for bugs, features and tech debt, with priority and effort, and documented the flow in a guide for the team.
3. Built AI skills that create issues and PRs already in the standard format, so nobody had to remember the rules.
4. Connected QA and blockers to Slack: when a task is ready for testing or gets blocked, the channel is notified.
5. Ran the daily and planned every fix and feature with the CTO, however small. I decided what went into each deploy and made sure it shipped.

## Result

Until January 2026, no merge came from a branch linked to an issue. From February to October there were about 100. Incidents in the standard format went from 0% to about 90% in five months, and I led more than 100 deploys in that period.

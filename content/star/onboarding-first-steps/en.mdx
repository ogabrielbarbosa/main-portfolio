## Situation

At LíderHub, customers paid and started using the platform with no guidance. The terms of use showed up only in a small banner, and without acceptance the company was legally exposed. The onboarding call, which teaches customers what to use and how, was a hidden button on the home screen, and customers didn't know they had to book it.

## Task

I designed and built a first login that secured terms acceptance and call booking without blocking customers who were already using the platform.

## Action

1. Made terms acceptance and call booking required steps of the first login. Customers can't move on without them.
2. Built a terms pop-up for existing customers. They can close it so their work isn't blocked, but it comes back on every new login until they accept.
3. Replaced the home screen with a First Steps page, with a 3-phase checklist: book onboarding, basic setup and integrations.
4. Protected the acceptance record with an atomic database operation, so a late event couldn't erase the accepted version. Kept the old function in production and shipped the new one alongside it.
5. When a customer got stuck on an endless loading screen on the terms step, I reproduced the bug on a fresh account, found the cause and fixed it the same day.

## Result

Before, in none of 16 weeks had more than 90% of new customers accepted the terms. In the 7 weeks after launch, 5 passed 90% for both acceptance and call booking, and the weekly median acceptance rose from ~76% to ~94%. Among existing customers, the pop-up raised accepted-terms coverage from ~45% to ~60%.

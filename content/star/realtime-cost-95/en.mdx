## Situation

In March 2026, with over a thousand customers on LíderHub, realtime produced ~80 million events in 2 days. Websocket latency hit 12 s and users kept refreshing. The database was ~300 GB on an 8XL instance, cost ~US$ 4.3k/month and was trending to ~US$ 6k.

## Task

Stabilize realtime and bring database cost down without stopping production.

## Action

1. Found an O(N²) keepalive: every client acknowledged every other client's ping. Replaced it with the SDK's native heartbeat in a Web Worker, with reconnect jitter.
2. Measured the traffic and found noisy neighbors: ~5% of tenants produced ~70% of the payload, and one incoming message fanned out to 4–8 inserts. Ruled out RLS with data: the slot without RLS was 8x slower.
3. Made triggers fire only on real changes and broadcasts carry only the changed fields.
4. Migrated 12 hooks from postgres_changes to broadcast.
5. Rewrote RLS for ~50 tables using JWT custom claims, with a rollback plan and a maintenance window. The function resolving the user's company was being called ~280 million times.
6. Replaced a stats reset with a 48-hour snapshot during the index cleanup. The fresh data reversed a decision: the index slated for removal was the one actually in use.

## Result

Broadcast events fell from ~40 million to ~2 million a day (−95%). Unused indexes on Message, Logs and Chat were removed with zero downtime, executed in production by Patrick.

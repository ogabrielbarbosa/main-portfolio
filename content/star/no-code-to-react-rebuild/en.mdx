## Situation

LíderHub is an AI CRM that helps law firms close contracts over WhatsApp. In 2025 the front end ran on no-code (WeWeb): it used over 3 GB of RAM, messages arrived late, the app was slow to load, and that was the main reason customers cancelled.

## Task

The team decided to rewrite the front end in React, with Supabase already as the backend. I owned the chat, messages, search and contacts and, above all, making everything fast enough to hold up under a customer base that kept growing.

## Action

1. Mapped the whole old app before writing code: every message type, format, assignment rule and tag. The new version couldn't lose anything customers already relied on.
2. Went after the weight. The old app reloaded the entire list on every new message. I rebuilt the chat with a lean list and an event-driven cache: a message arrives, only its conversation changes.
3. Made realtime respect the filters on screen: a conversation only enters the list if it matches the open filter, with no reload.
4. Loaded messages in pages. The chat opens with the 20 most recent and fetches older ones as you scroll up. To find and jump to an old message without reading the whole history, I built two-way pagination: <ProjectLink href="/en/projects/chat-message-search">Message search optimization</ProjectLink>.
5. After launch, I stayed on the front line until the chat stopped breaking: tracked down the cause of every urgent bug and merged the work of 3 developers into one release.

## Result

Urgent bugs dropped from 20 to 1 a week in about 4 weeks. Opening a conversation went from about 3 seconds in the old app to instant. And the same chat held up while the customer base grew from ~450 to over 1,100.

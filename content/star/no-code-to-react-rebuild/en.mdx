## Situation

LíderHub is an AI CRM that helps law firms close contracts over WhatsApp. In 2025 the app ran on no-code (Bubble + WeWeb). It used over 3 GB of RAM, froze the browser, delivered messages late, and customers were cancelling.

## Task

The team decided to rewrite it in React + Supabase. I owned the whole support experience: chat, messages, search, kanban and contacts. We had about 5 weeks to launch, much of it working on-site with the team in Florianópolis.

## Action

1. Mapped the entire old app before writing code: message types, formatting, assignment rules, tags and logs.
2. Rebuilt the chat with a lean list and an event-driven cache, instead of refetching everything on each message.
3. Made realtime respect filters, together with Pedro: an event only enters the list if it matches the active filter.
4. Led stabilization after the August 2, 2025 launch: traced the chat that closed on its own, fixed a blank-screen crash within a day, and merged 8 branches from 3 developers on August 26.
5. Built bidirectional pagination: jumping to an old message loads only its page and its neighbors. I built visual debug panels to get the logic right.

## Result

Urgent bugs dropped from 20 to 1 per week in about 4 weeks. Today over a thousand customers run on this chat.

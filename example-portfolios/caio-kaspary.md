## 4. What I believe

*Philosophies, Frameworks, and Modus Operandi related to work.*

### ▸ Give First, but say No.

I always help people without any expectation of getting anything back, but not everyone.

Giving first means spending time to help someone, generating value, organizing feedback, and showing examples, but not everyone is ready to receive that.

- [Give First - Techstars](https://www.techstars.com/blog/podcasts/what-is-give-first)
- [Givers and Takers](https://www.amazon.com/Give-Take-Helping-Others-Success/dp/0143124986)
- [The art of saying no](https://www.amazon.com/Art-Saying-NO-Reclaim-Granted/dp/1549572741)

### ▸ KISS - Keep it Simple, Stupid

It is extremely useful for making decisions during product development. The simpler the design of a structure, a workflow, or a process, the more likely it is to work better, measure more appropriately, be maintained, and make improvements.

- [KISS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KISS_principle)

### ▸ DRY - Don't repeat yourself

There are easy/fast ways to build products, and there are efficient ways. Developers who think short-term just want to deliver tasks and don't consider maintaining the product.

I always focus on smart flows and structure and facilitate for future team members. That means avoiding repetitive workflows, elements, and designs, and building custom and reusable things in a smart and organized way.

- [Don't repeat yourself](https://zapier.com/blog/dont-repeat-yourself)

### ▸ Do things that don't scale

This is one of the most robust frameworks I follow in order to build products. It's just cheaper/smarter to validate first and make sure we are going in the right direction, so then we automate/develop further things.

![](https://ckaspary.notion.site/image/https%3A%2F%2Fprod-files-secure.s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2F47d27bdb-dd04-462d-8ecb-0e5a0b249dd4%2Fc5e7a130-ca4d-42aa-9432-6157ac11ba02%2Fdo-things-that-scale-funders-and-founders-inc42-754x1024.png?table=block&id=db7f83e9-27cd-4b56-8a46-cdd0d29bcd59&cache=v2)
